Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The Renatti Group wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control.

Our goal

We build this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. That includes readable text contrast, keyboard access to every link and form field, clear headings, labeled form fields and a "Skip to content" link.

Where we are

We test our pages ourselves with automated checkers and by using the keyboard alone. This site has not yet had a third-party audit, so there may be problems we have missed.

Tell us about a problem

If you have trouble using any part of this site, email renatti.group@gmail.com with the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 2 business days and to fix the problem or offer you another way to get the information, such as a phone call or an email.
