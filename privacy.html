Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The Renatti Group ("we", "us") builds websites for small businesses in New Jersey. This policy explains what information this website collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form details: your name, email address, phone number (optional), business name (optional) and the message you write.
  • Emails and calls: if you email or call us, we keep a record of the message, your contact details and what we discussed.
  • Basic server data: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type under its own privacy statement.

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or social media pixels on this site, and we do not set cookies ourselves.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare a quote.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with you so we can follow up.
  • To deliver and support work you hire us to do.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the services that help us run the business, such as our email provider and our contact form provider, and when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for up to 3 years, and client records for as long as we work together and as long as the law requires us to keep business and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email renatti.group@gmail.com and we will respond within 45 days. If you do not want email or calls from us, tell us and we will stop.

Calls and text messages

We only call or text the number you give us about your request. We do not send marketing texts unless you ask for them.

Children

This site is for business owners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
